The SimplePractice Self-Billing Masterclass

The Guide That Makes SimplePractice SIMPLE

A step-by-step course for solo and new mental health practice owners — from credentialing through claims to a repeatable monthly admin routine.

7 modules · 30 lessons · lessons unlock as they're added

Three paths through this course: your path depends on how your practice bills, and each one is just as complete a course as the others — none is a stripped-down version.

Self-pay only? Skip Module 4 (Insurance Billing) and Module 5 (Automation) entirely — those are tagged Insurance track below. Your path: Modules 0 → 1 (skip EDI/ERA) → 2 → 3 → 6.

Insurance only? Skip Module 3 (Self-Pay Billing) entirely — it's tagged Self-pay track below. Your path: Modules 0 → 1 (full) → 2 → 4 → 5 → 6.

Mixed practice (both)? Take all 7 modules, in order.
